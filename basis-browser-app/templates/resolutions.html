Joint Resolutions

HJR/SJR. Used for constitutional amendments and memorials to Congress. Substantive — require both chambers' adoption but do not become statute.

Concurrent Resolutions — Substantive

HCR/SCR on policy or study matters (task forces, official requests, commemorations). Not procedural housekeeping.

Concurrent Resolutions — Procedural (Uniform Rules)

HCR/SCR that suspend or amend the chambers' Uniform Rules. Pure plumbing — most are titled SUSPEND UNIFORM RULES FOR SB X and exist solely to waive deadline rules so a paired bill can be acted on faster. Excluded from the main pipeline, activity, and bill-progress views.
