Pipeline Status

At Governor — Action Deadline Countdown

Per Article II §17 of the Alaska Constitution, the governor has 15 days during session and 20 days after adjournment to act on bills received. The deadline per bill below is calculated against whichever window applied on the bill's transmittal date.

Conference, Concur, or Die

Bills that have crossed over, been amended, and returned to their origin chamber. The originating chamber must concur with the amendments, refuse to concur (forcing a conference committee), or let the bill die.

Conference Committees

Bills where a conference committee has been appointed. These are actively being negotiated between House and Senate conferees.
